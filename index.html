The Free Online Drawing & Guessing Game You Play With Friends

GuessArt is a browser-based multiplayer drawing and guessing game — no download, no account, no waiting room full of strangers. Create a private room, drop the link in your group chat, and start drawing in seconds. It's the free skribbl.io alternative and gartic.io alternative built for playing with the people you actually know.

How to Play GuessArt

  1. Create a Room. Pick a name, hit "Create Room," and get an instant 6-character room code.
  2. Invite Your Friends. Share the room code or link — they join in one tap, no sign-up required.
  3. Take Turns Drawing. One player draws a secret word on the canvas while everyone else races to guess.
  4. Guess & Score Points. Type your guesses in the chat — the faster you guess, the more points you score.
  5. Talk Trash Live. Flip on voice chat and blast a meme soundboard voiceline when someone's drawing goes sideways.

Why Players Choose GuessArt

🔒 Private Rooms

Every game runs in a private, code-protected room — play a real draw and guess game online with just your friend group, not random strangers.

🎙️ Live Voice Chat

Built-in voice chat lets you hear every reaction in real time, so guessing wrong is half the fun.

Game Rules

One player draws a secret word on the canvas while everyone else races to type the correct guess in chat.

Guessing faster earns more points — the timer counts down each turn, so quicker correct guesses score higher than late ones.

Turns rotate automatically between players. Over multiple rounds, the player with the highest total score at the end wins.

The drawer can't type guesses during their own turn, and typing the secret word in chat while someone else is drawing doesn't count — that's on the honor system between friends.

Playing vs Computer follows the same scoring, just against the built-in bot instead of a second player.

Privacy Policy

GuessArt doesn't require an account, so it doesn't collect your name, email, or any personal identity — the nickname you type is local to your device and shared only with the friend in your room.

Gameplay (drawing strokes, chat, voice) travels directly between you and your friend's device using a peer-to-peer connection — there's no central server storing or recording your games.

Voice chat requires microphone permission from your browser. It's used only for the live call with your friend and is never recorded or uploaded anywhere.

If you use the Contact Us form, the message and optional email you provide are sent directly to the developer's inbox via your own mail app — nothing is stored in a database.
